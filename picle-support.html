Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Every level shows a row of emoji describing a film, book, fairy tale or legend. The category is shown above the emoji. Type your answer into the letter slots using the keyboard at the bottom. When every slot is filled the answer is checked automatically, with a brief pause so you can correct a mistake first.

What are hint tokens, and how do I get more?

Tokens pay for hints. You earn them by finishing levels — more for solving without help — and by opening Picle each day. You can also watch a short ad for more, or unlock Pro for unlimited hints. Nothing is ever taken from you for a wrong answer.

What do the different hints do?

Reveal a letter fills one correct slot. Clear the keyboard greys out several letters that are not in the answer. Give me a clue shows a written hint about the answer. Skip passes the level entirely, and is paid for by watching an ad rather than with tokens.

I am completely stuck on a level. What now?

After five wrong answers on the same level, Picle offers you a free skip — no tokens, no ad. Being stuck should not be the reason you stop playing.

Why was my answer rejected when I think it is right?

Answers use letters and spaces only, and the slots show exactly how many letters each word has. Some levels accept more than one answer, and some recognise a near miss and tell you how close you were. If you have found an answer that really should be accepted, email danishk7809@gmail.com and it will be added.

Can I make my own levels?

Yes. Tap the photo button on the main screen. You can build a level from one of your own photos, which Picle hides for you automatically, or from a row of emoji you choose yourself. The free version keeps three saved levels; Pro is unlimited.

Are my custom levels shared with anyone?

No. Levels you create stay on your device. There is no sharing, no upload, and no account. Deleting the app removes them.

Does Picle need access to my photos?

Only to build your own levels from your pictures, and it asks the first time you open that screen rather than on launch. The game is completely playable without granting it. Limited access — where you pick a few photos rather than allowing all of them — is fully supported.

What does Picle Pro include?

Unlimited hints, no ads of any kind, and unlimited custom levels. It is a single payment, not a subscription — nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Every level is playable free either way; Pro sells convenience, not content.

Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Yes. Open Settings or the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account used originally.

Does Picle work offline?

Yes. The whole game runs on your device. Only ads and purchases need a connection.

Will there be more levels?

Yes. When you finish the last one you will find a screen inviting you to send feedback, and it is read. What gets added next depends largely on what people say there.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Danish Khan operates the Picle iOS application ("App") and how information is handled when you use it.

1. There Is No Account and No Server

Picle has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud component. Your progress, your hint tokens, your settings and any levels you create are stored on your device and nowhere else. There is no server to send them to.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or passwords.

3. Your Photos Never Leave Your Device

If you choose to build a level from one of your photos, that photo is read, resized and stored inside the App's own folder on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted or sent anywhere. Photo processing — including the automatic cutout used for silhouette levels — happens entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. We cannot see your photos.

4. Local Device Storage

The following is stored locally to make the App work:

  • Which levels you have completed and how far you have unlocked
  • Your hint token balance and when you last claimed a daily bonus
  • Levels you have created, including any photo you supplied for them
  • Your sound, haptics and appearance settings
  • Your purchase status, so the App knows what you own without checking the network every time
  • Anonymous play statistics — attempts per level, hints used, levels left unfinished — used to find badly designed levels. This never leaves the device and is visible to you on a hidden statistics screen in the App

All of it is under your control and is removed when you delete the App.

5. Photo Library and Camera Permission

Picle asks for photo or camera access only when you first open the custom level creator, never on launch. Access is used solely to let you choose a picture for a level of your own. If you grant limited access, Picle sees only the photos you selected. You can change or revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — displays ads to users who have not purchased Pro. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for advertising and measurement. See Google's privacy policy. Ads are disabled entirely for Pro users. No photo data and no gameplay data is ever shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Apple StoreKit — used for the in-app purchase. Apple processes payment data in accordance with their own policies. We never see your payment details.
  • Apple Analytics — Apple may provide aggregated analytics, diagnostics or crash information if you have opted in to sharing with developers.

7. How Information Is Used

  • Remember your progress, tokens and settings
  • Store and play the levels you create
  • Show you which levels are proving too hard, so they can be improved
  • Process the Pro purchase through Apple StoreKit
  • Serve advertising to users on the free tier

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your photos, your progress or your created levels with anyone. Third-party providers may process limited technical data as described above.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through a third-party service, please contact the relevant provider.

10. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by iOS's own security and your device passcode. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but Picle minimises risk by not transmitting anything in the first place.

11. Your Choices

  • Revoke or limit photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos
  • Limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Purchase Pro to remove all advertising
  • Delete all locally stored data by removing the App from your device

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes become effective when the updated version is posted, and the effective date above will reflect when changes were made.
