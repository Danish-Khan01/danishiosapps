Paperwink Privacy Policy

This policy describes data handling in Paperwink: Sticky Widget Notes, provided by Danish Khan.

Effective date: 2 November 2026

At a glance

Your note content is stored on your device and may sync or be shared through Apple’s iCloud services. Paperwink does not send your notes to a developer-operated notes server. Advertising services may process device and usage information. If you contact support, we receive what you send us.

1. Notes, photos, drawings and styles

Paperwink stores the words, checklist items, completion states, photos, ink, styles, widget choices and related note settings needed to provide its features. The app and its widget extension share saved data through an Apple App Group on your device. My styles recipes are stored locally. Widget previews and exports use your selected note data.

Image-based text recognition uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device. Paperwink does not upload images to a developer-operated OCR service. Apple Pencil Scribble is provided by the operating system and follows your device settings.

2. iCloud and sharing

When available, iCloud sync stores note information through Apple’s CloudKit services associated with your Apple Account. Invited-note sharing sends the selected note and its attachments through CloudKit to people you choose. Apple processes this data under its own terms and Privacy Policy. Shared recipients and destinations you export to may retain their own copies. Do not share sensitive information with recipients you do not trust.

3. Permissions and optional features

  • Photos: the system photo picker provides images you select for a note.
  • Location: if you enable a place-triggered note, Paperwink uses the location and region you select to trigger notes on arrival or departure. Background triggers require appropriate system permission. Selected trigger settings can be stored with the note and synced with it.
  • Notifications: permission allows the app to schedule reminders you request.
  • Biometric authentication: supported system authentication can protect access to locked notes. Paperwink does not receive your biometric templates.

You can review or revoke permissions in system Settings. Disabling a permission may stop its related feature.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Paperwink includes Google’s Mobile Ads SDK for advertising. According to Google’s iOS data disclosure, the SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, crash and performance information, device or app-scoped identifiers, advertising data and interactions with the app or ads. These may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and service performance. Actual processing depends on SDK behaviour, ad availability and applicable device permissions.

Paperwink does not supply your note text, selected photos or drawings as ad-request content. Device advertising permissions and Apple’s tracking controls affect access to advertising identifiers.

See Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s partner information and Google’s iOS advertising data disclosure.

5. Purchases

Available purchases and subscriptions use Apple’s StoreKit system. Paperwink receives transaction and entitlement information needed to verify access and restore purchases. Apple handles payment details; Paperwink does not receive your payment-card information. Manage subscriptions and billing through your Apple Account.

6. Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue. Please include only information you intend to share. We may retain correspondence as needed to handle requests, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence we hold.

7. Retention and your choices

You can edit or delete notes using the app and manage iCloud and device backups through Apple’s settings. Removing the app deletes its local container, but does not necessarily remove iCloud data, shared recipients’ copies, exports or backups. These must be managed separately. Support cannot remotely delete local-only note contents.

You can decline optional permissions, avoid sharing notes, and choose which information to include in a support request. Depending on your location, privacy law may provide rights regarding personal information held about you. Contact us about information held by us, and use Apple’s or Google’s channels for information they process independently.

8. Security, children and processing locations

Device storage and Apple services use the protections provided by the operating system and service. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Apple, Google, GitHub and email providers may process information in countries other than your own under their policies.

Paperwink is a general-purpose notes app. We do not ask children to submit personal information to support. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can address the request.

9. These web pages

These support and policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no developer-added analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver the pages. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External sites and your email provider follow their own privacy practices.

10. Updates and contact

The effective date identifies this policy version. It may be updated as Paperwink and its data handling change.
