2. What the App Does

Storage Cleaner helps you review and delete photos and videos from your device's photo library. Marking a photo in the App does not delete it. Marked items are collected in a pending bin, and are only removed when you choose to delete them and confirm the system prompt that iOS displays.

Deleted photos are moved to the Recently Deleted album in Apple's Photos app, where they remain for 30 days before being permanently removed by iOS. Restoring them during that period is done through the Photos app.
