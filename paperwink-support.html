Common questions

How do I create and save a note?

Choose Create a note, select a note type and size, then write, add checklist items, choose a photo or draw. Use Done to save. Reopen a saved note from the library to continue editing.

How do I put a note on my Home Screen or Lock Screen?

Save the note and use its Pin to widget action. Add Paperwink through your device’s widget gallery, then edit the widget to select a note. Available widget sizes and Lock Screen controls depend on the device and system version.

Do completed tasks update between the widget and app?

Supported Home Screen checklist widgets let you mark tasks complete. The widget and app use the same saved note. Refreshes are scheduled by the system and may take a moment. If a widget looks stale, open Paperwink and check its selected note and saved state.

How do I design a style or return to plain notes?

Open More → Create your style. Choose paper, fonts, text finishes, stickers, frames and background patterns. Layers controls their order and opacity. My styles saves reusable designs. Reset to default restores a plain style, with Undo available. Apply changes the draft; save the note to keep it.

Why does a widget look different from the editor?

Adapt reflows the note for the actual widget size. Keep original fits the authored canvas. Widget preview also offers independent family layout settings. Shared text and style edits affect the current note draft; save the note to update its widgets.

What is the difference between Draw & Write, Scribble and OCR?

Draw & Write preserves editable PencilKit ink. On a supported iPad with Scribble enabled, Write as Text enters editable words in the selected font. Recognize text reads a photo or a rendered drawing using image-based OCR and lets you review the result. Dedicated PencilKit stroke-to-text conversion is not provided. Recognition accuracy varies.

How do I restore lifetime access?

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Paperwink Plus is a one-time lifetime purchase and does not renew. The price shown by Apple is local to your storefront. For billing and refunds, visit Apple’s purchase support. Do not send us payment-card details or your Apple Account password.

Why are location notes or reminders not appearing?

Check Paperwink’s notification and location permissions in system Settings. Background place triggers need the appropriate background location permission. Focus settings, connectivity and system widget refresh scheduling can also affect when a reminder or widget appears.

Where are my notes stored?

Notes are stored on your device. iCloud sync and invited-note sharing use Apple’s CloudKit services when available. Saved designs in My styles are stored locally. Before deleting the app or troubleshooting missing data, preserve notes and images you need. Support cannot remotely recover local-only notes.
