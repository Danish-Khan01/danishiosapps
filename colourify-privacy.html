Colourify: Fill Colour

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Danish Khan ("we," "us," "our"), the developer of Colourify for iPhone and iPad (the "App"), handles information. Colourify is a colouring app made for children. Colouring, drawings and personalisation are processed on your device. Parents and guardians can optionally contact us for support.

In short, for parents

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are included in Colourify.
  • Colouring works offline. We do not automatically receive your child's name, drawings, photos or colouring activity.
  • The updated App replaces the in-app Google Form with Email Support behind a parental gate. It opens your email app; nothing is sent automatically.
  • If you send an email, we receive your sender address and the information you choose to include. Support is optional and is not needed to use the App.

1. Optional Parent Support

Email Support is intended for parents and guardians. After completing the parental gate, you can open a draft addressed to goodwork7809@gmail.com with the subject "Colourify support". You choose what to write and whether to send it in your own email app. The App does not automatically add a message body, device model, iOS version, app version, identifiers, artwork or attachments. If no email app opens, you can copy the support address and use your preferred email service.

When you send us an email, we receive your email address, any sender name supplied by your email service, your message and anything you choose to attach, along with normal email delivery information. We use correspondence to reply, investigate problems and consider feature requests. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or marketing, and do not sell personal information. Please do not include your child's personal information or sensitive information.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

  • Colouring progress and artwork — region colours, brush strokes, action history used for time-lapse replay, finished pictures, and saved drawings.
  • Imported drawings — photos you select or scan to make colouring pages, and original drawings you choose to keep.
  • Optional personalisation — a first name, colouring preferences, favourite subjects and preferred time of day, used locally for greetings, suggestions and reminders. You can change or clear these in Settings → Personalise.
  • My Art journal and settings — colouring activity, badges, sound and appearance settings, and local purchase status.

We do not automatically receive this information. The home-screen widget reads an on-device snapshot shared with the App. Saved or shared exports and device backups are explained below.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera and document scanning — used when you choose to photograph or scan a drawing; the App processes it on the device.
  • Photo picker — iOS provides the photo you select rather than giving the App access to your whole library.
  • Saving to Photos — requested when you choose to save an image or video.
  • Notifications — optional daily reminders scheduled locally, enabled behind a parental gate.

You can manage applicable permissions in iOS Settings.

4. In-App Purchase

Colourify offers an optional one-time purchase, with buying and restoring protected by a parental gate. Apple processes payment. The App uses StoreKit to check purchase status; we do not receive your card details or Apple ID through the App.

5. Email Providers, Sharing and External Links

Support correspondence is handled through your chosen email provider and our Gmail mailbox, provided by Google. These providers process email and associated delivery information under their own policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. Email may be processed outside your country. Opening a draft does not send it; sending is your choice.

If a grown-up saves or shares a picture, drawing, colouring book or time-lapse through the system share sheet, the chosen destination receives that export. Its privacy policy applies. The App does not automatically upload artwork to us. External links, including the App Store and policy pages, are protected by a parental gate.

6. Advertising and Tracking

Colourify contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier, track users across apps or websites, or create advertising profiles.

7. Children's Privacy

Colourify is designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Personalisation is stored locally. Email Support is for parents and guardians, behind a parental gate. A parental gate is not a substitute for any legally required parental consent.

Please do not send children's personal information. If you believe we have received it, contact us so we can investigate and delete it. We will obtain any legally required consent before introducing a feature that intentionally collects children's personal information.

8. Deleting Local Data and Backups

You can remove saved drawings and progress using the App's controls and change or clear personalisation in Settings. Uninstalling the App removes its local app-container data; exports saved elsewhere and existing backups may remain. If you use iCloud Backup or computer backups, App data may be included under your device's backup settings. We cannot access those backups. Manage them through Apple or your backup tools.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information. Contact goodwork7809@gmail.com with your request. We may need enough information to locate the correspondence and verify the request. Deleting the App does not delete emails or feedback already received by us.

10. Security, Retention and Earlier Versions

We restrict access to support correspondence and retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed to handle requests, investigate issues or meet legal obligations. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed. You can request deletion at the address above. We may retain feature ideas after removing identifying details. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Earlier App versions may still offer the optional parent-gated Google Form. If you use that older form and press Send, it submits the feedback category and message, any optional email you enter, and the app name, version, build, bundle identifier, iOS version and device model. It does not automatically attach artwork or a unique device identifier. Google processes submissions and network information under its privacy policy, and responses may remain in our Google Form or linked response spreadsheet. Those responses follow the same support purposes, retention and deletion process described here. Update the App to use Email Support instead.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date identifies the latest revision. We will obtain consent where legally required before introducing changes to how personal information is handled.
