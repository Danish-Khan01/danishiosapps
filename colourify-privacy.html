Colourify: Fill Colour

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Danish Khan ("we," "us," "our"), the developer of the Colourify: Fill Colour app for iPhone and iPad (the "App"), handles information. Colourify is a colouring app made for children. Colouring, drawings and personalisation stay on your device. A parent or guardian can optionally send us feedback through a separate, parent-gated form. This policy explains that choice and the information sent with it.

In short, for parents

  • We do not automatically collect your child's name, drawings, photos or colouring activity. We do not sell personal information.
  • The App has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Optional parent feedback is delivered through Google Forms.
  • Colouring works offline. Sending parent feedback requires an internet connection and happens only when a grown-up presses Send.
  • Buying, restoring purchases, leaving the App, sharing, turning on reminders and accessing parent feedback are protected by a grown-up question (a parental gate). Feedback is optional, and the email field can be left blank.

1. Information We Collect

We do not automatically receive colouring activity or the information stored locally by the App. If a parent or guardian chooses to send feedback, we receive:

  • Feedback category and message — for example a feature request, a bug report or an idea.
  • Optional email address — only if you enter it, so we can reply. No email is required to send an idea.
  • Technical context — the app name, app version and build, app bundle identifier, iOS version and device model. This helps us understand and investigate your message. The app does not attach an advertising identifier or a unique device identifier.

These fields are sent only when you press Send. The form does not automatically attach drawings, photos, colouring progress, personalisation answers or your child's name. Please do not include your child's name, contact details or other sensitive information in the message.

We use submissions to respond to parents, investigate problems and improve Colourify. We do not use them for advertising, profiling or marketing.

2. Information Stored Only On Your Device

To work, the App keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad. It stays there, under your control, and is never sent to us:

  • Colouring progress — the colours filled into each picture, brush strokes, and finished pictures.
  • Your own drawings — photos you choose to import (from the camera or your photo library) to turn into colouring pages, and drawings you choose to keep in My Art.
  • Optional personalisation — if you choose to answer the optional questions: a first name, a colouring style, favourite subjects and a preferred time of day. These are used only on the device, to show a greeting, title a colouring book and set the reminder time. They are never required, and can be changed or cleared at any time in Settings → Personalise.
  • My Art journal — which days pictures were coloured and the badges earned.
  • Settings and purchase status — for example sound, dark mode and whether the one-time purchase has been made.

The home-screen widget and, if installed, the App's Messages stickers read this same on-device data. Deleting the App removes all of it.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera — only when you choose to photograph a paper drawing. The photo stays on the device.
  • Photos (add only) — only to save a picture or video you choose to save. The App cannot see or read your other photos.
  • Photo picker — when you import a photo, iOS shares only the one photo you pick. No permission to your library is granted.
  • Notifications — optional daily reminders, created on the device (no server is involved). They can only be turned on by a grown-up, behind the parental gate.

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Colourify.

4. In-App Purchase

The App offers one optional, one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details, name, email or Apple ID — only confirmation, on the device, that the purchase exists. Purchases are protected by a parental gate.

5. Sharing and Links

We do not sell or rent personal information. Optional parent feedback is sent over HTTPS to Google Forms and stored in our Google Form and, if linked, its response spreadsheet. Google processes the submission to provide that service and may process network information such as your IP address under its Privacy Policy. Google may process information on servers outside your country. We can access the responses to provide support and improve the App. Responses are not published in the App.

If a grown-up chooses to save or share a picture using the iOS share sheet, it goes where they choose, and that app or service's own privacy policy applies. Links that leave the App (to this page, our Terms, or the App Store) are behind the parental gate.

6. Advertising and Tracking

The App contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics software. It does not track users across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not build profiles of anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

Colourify is designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. The optional personalisation first name is stored only on the device and is not included in feedback. The feedback feature is intended for parents and guardians, is protected by a parental gate and requires an explicit Send action. A parental gate is not a substitute for legally required parental consent.

Please do not submit personal information about a child. If you believe a submission contains a child's personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it. We will not introduce a feature that intentionally collects children's personal information without first updating this policy and obtaining any consent required by applicable law.

Parents and guardians can review or remove the on-device information the App keeps: change or clear the name in Settings → Personalise, delete drawings and kept pictures inside the App, or delete the App to remove its local data. Feedback already submitted must be deleted separately as explained below. If you have any concern, contact us at the address below.

8. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the App's on-device data in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to them.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict its use. To ask about or request deletion of a feedback response, email the contact address below. Include enough information to locate the response, such as its approximate submission date and subject; if you supplied an email, you can contact us from that address. We may need to verify that a request relates to your submission. A response sent without an email may be harder to identify.

You can delete locally stored App data on your device. Deleting the App does not delete feedback already sent to us; contact us separately to request that deletion.

10. Security and Retention

Colouring, drawings, personalisation and journal data stay on your device, protected by iOS, until you delete them or delete the App, subject to your device backups.

Submitted parent feedback is stored in our Google Form and any linked response spreadsheet, and may also be retained in support correspondence if we reply. We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, investigate an issue or meet legal obligations, and delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed. You can request earlier deletion using the contact details below. We may keep feature ideas after removing identifying details. We use HTTPS for submission and restrict access to feedback; no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Effective Date" above shows the latest version. If we change how personal information is handled, we will update this page and obtain consent where required before introducing the change.
