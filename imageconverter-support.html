How do I convert a single photo?

Open the Single tab, tap to choose a photo from your library, select the format you want to convert to, adjust quality if the format supports it, then tap Convert. Once it's done, save it to Photos, save it to Files, or share it directly.

How does batch conversion work?

Open the Batch tab and select multiple photos at once. Choose one output format for the whole batch, tap Convert All, and track progress for each image individually. When everything's done, save the whole batch to Photos or Files in one go.

Why did my file size increase after converting to PNG or TIFF?

PNG and TIFF can produce larger files than JPEG or HEIC because they use different compression. Converting a previously compressed photo to a lossless format does not recover detail already discarded by the source format. Use an adjustable-quality format or resizing when a smaller file is the priority.

Where do my converted photos go?

After converting, you can save directly to your Photos library, export to the Files app or iCloud Drive, or share to any other app via the share sheet. Your last 20 conversions are also kept in the Recent tab so you can save or share them again without redoing the conversion.

What does "Keep metadata" do?

Photos often contain metadata like the date taken, camera details, and sometimes GPS location. Turning this toggle off strips that information from the converted file — useful if you're sharing a photo publicly and don't want location data attached.

Does the app upload my photos anywhere?

No. Every conversion happens entirely on your device. The App does not upload your selected photos for conversion, and no account or sign-up is required. Sharing or saving through a cloud provider uses that service.

What can I use for free?

The free version includes five images per local day, all supported output formats, resizing, file-size targets, and original-quality PDF export. Batch conversion is available within your free allowance. Optional rewarded ads can unlock additional images. Pro unlocks unlimited images, removes ads, and adds PDF compression and ZIP export.

How do I make a smaller PDF?

Select PDF, then choose Balanced or Small file compression with Pro in Single or Batch. Original quality remains available free. Compression preserves the selected page layout and image proportions; results depend on your source images and settings.

Does “Aim for under…” guarantee an exact file size?

No. Choose JPEG, HEIC, WebP, or AVIF and a target size; the app aims for that limit using adjustable compression. Some targets require smaller image dimensions. In Batch, the target applies to each image. Formats without adjustable compression offer resizing instead.

What does Lifetime Pro include?

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads, unlocks unlimited images, enables Balanced and Small file PDF compression in Single and Batch, and enables ZIP export. Lifetime access includes all future app updates. There is no subscription.

The price is shown in your App Store currency before you buy and may vary by country or region. Existing lifetime purchasers keep their access.

Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

Yes. Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings or on the paywall, using the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.
